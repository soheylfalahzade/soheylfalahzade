<p align="center">
I design and build adaptive, ML-driven traffic control systems that combine deep-learning perception with classical control theory (Max-Pressure, Fuzzy Logic). My M.Sc. thesis at Yazd University tackles real-time emergency-vehicle prioritization at urban intersections, and this profile collects the open-source work that supports it.
</p>

---

### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Adaptive Traffic Signal Control** — Max-Pressure control combined with fuzzy inference for green-time decisions
- **Vehicle Detection & Multi-Object Tracking** — YOLOv8 / ByteTrack pipelines for occlusion-robust vehicle counting
- **Emergency Vehicle Prioritization** — transfer-learning classifiers for detecting priority fleets in dense traffic
- **Simulation & Digital Twins** — CARLA + SUMO co-simulation for closed-loop evaluation of control policies

📄 Thesis: *Adaptive Traffic Signal Management with ML-Driven Fleet Prioritization*, Yazd University (2024–2027), advised by Dr. Mohammad Farshi & Dr. Saeed Alikhani.
